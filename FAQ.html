Meet Your DM Team
Question: Where are you located?

Answer: We are located at Chaos Games and More at 4065 Club Manor Dr, Pueblo, CO 81008 on sundays. Games start at 5pm and end at 9pm.

Question: What is the difference between the Unwoven Lands and the Shattered Lands?


Answer: The Unwoven Lands is a traditional fantasy setting, similar to those in Lord of the Rings or Game of Thrones, whereas the Shattered Lands is a Sci-Fi setting similar to Warhammer 40k, Cyberpunk, or Star Wars.

Question: What levels are character tiers?

Answer: Tier 1 play is levels 1-4, Tier 2 is 5-10, Tier 3 is 11-16, and Tier 4 is 17-20.

Question: How do we level up?

Answer: At the end of every week during a season, every player will recieve 10 "Downtime Days". These are a currency which you can spend to level up your character, obtain magic items, and investigate information revealed in a previous session. Spend 15 downtime days to gain 1 level-up.

Question: Where can I sign up for games?

Answer: By joining our community discord server, you are able to sign up for games early rather than walking in and finding an available seat. Games are posted every Thursday night.
